How to read this map

Normal navigates and invokes commands. Insert types text. Visual acts on a selection. Operator-pending is the interval after an operator such as d or c. Command-line is the :, / and ? prompt. A mapping marked Local is explicitly present in the captured config; LazyVim defaults are kept separate. Plugin mappings may only exist after that plugin loads.
